Website file: lounge.mp3. Converted to 96 kbps MP3 with a gentle 9 kHz low-pass filter for background playback. The website provides independent music and effects volume controls and lowers background music while a portfolio project is open.

Current interaction sounds are synthesized with Web Audio in app/useStudioAudio.ts: soft sine tones, gentle amplitude envelopes and an 850 Hz low-pass filter. The older Freesound recordings remain archived in this directory but are no longer used by the interface.
